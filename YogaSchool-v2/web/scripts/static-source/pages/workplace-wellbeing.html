What is yoga anyway? Yoga is for the mind and, as they say, the body is not stiff, the mind is!

Yoga dates back 5000 years and is non-religious, more of a belief system. The healing properties of yoga are being noted more and more, with some doctors prescribing yoga for depression, anxiety and stress. Health funds are also starting to recognise the remedial powers of yoga.

Our qualified and fully insured yoga teachers can come to you, or you can bring your staff to our large studio in Piccones Shopping Village on Pease Street, Edge Hill. We can tailor a session or create an ongoing wellbeing program to suit your organisation’s individual requirements.
