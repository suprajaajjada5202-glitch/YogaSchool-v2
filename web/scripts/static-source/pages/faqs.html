FAQs

Good to know

Everything you need before your first class, from what to wear to parking and pregnancy yoga.

Beginners welcome

I don't have a yoga mat or equipment.

Included in your class passes at The Yoga School are all the mats and equipment (props) that you will need. So all you need to bring is a towel and a bottle of water. All of our equipment is of the highest studio quality with large mats for you to feel comfortable on.

What is parking like?

The parking is free and some of it is undercover. There are lots of options at Piccones Shopping Village. From parking your car to being in the studio the average time is less than 60 seconds, and no parking wardens :)

I'm pregnant. Should I do yoga?

Our teachers are trained in pregnancy yoga and we have many students who attend public classes while pregnant. Please let the teacher know if you are pregnant when signing in. We also offer private pregnancy yoga sessions.

My back is stiff and my hips hurt. Should I do yoga?

Many students have exactly these same issues and enjoy the way that yoga opens the body up to let the energy flow, which helps to ease these pains. If you have severe conditions please call us to make a free appointment to help us understand your challenges.

I'm not really into group classes. What are my options?

We offer private sessions and can also cater for small private groups.

I have trouble climbing stairs.

Our studio is street level with easy access for both able and differently abled students. We are fully wheelchair accessible.

If I am late, will the door be locked?

No. But please plan to arrive ten minutes before class starts. We understand not every day runs to plan. If you arrive after class has started, please be mindful of the other students, quietly find a mat and enjoy your practice. The door will be open.

What do I wear?

Men: loose-fitting shorts and a t-shirt or singlet. Women: active wear works, and so do yoga pants.

What if I need to go to the toilet during class?

No problem, this is not uncommon in class due to the movements your body will do. Quietly move off your mat and carefully walk to the toilet.

How about eating before class?

A light snack 1 or 2 hours before class should be OK, and it always depends on the student.

What is the history of yoga?

Yoga started around 5000 years ago in Northern India, which is why the poses (Asanas) are pronounced in both English and Sanskrit.

Is yoga religious?

Yoga is non-religious, more of a belief system. Many of the world's religious leaders practise yoga, and so do many successful business leaders. They say it helps with making clear and non-emotional decisions during their day.

How do I become a yoga teacher?

Firstly you must practise yoga and have a keen interest in knowledge development. We run teacher training courses with our Knoff Yoga partners. Teacher training varies from one week to one month and is all conducted at our well-equipped, fully air-conditioned Cairns studio at Piccones Shopping Village on Pease Street.
